How Elizabethtown Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a spring rinse of the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so small issues get caught early.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix.
